STAGE 4: EXPLICIT INSTRUCTION - Teach Time

⏱ 10:00

In Python, standard math signs look a bit different from math class. Study the table below as the teacher explains.

Operation Math Class Python Syntax
Addition / Subtraction +, - +, -
Multiplication × or • * (Asterisk)
Division ÷ or fraction / (Forward Slash)
⚠️ CRITICAL MISCONCEPTION: The = symbol does NOT mean "equals". It is an Assignment Operator. It computes the math on the right side and stores it in the variable on the left side.
